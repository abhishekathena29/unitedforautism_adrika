## Full description (max 4000)

United For Autism is a warm, judgement-free community for parents, caregivers and families of autistic children. Ask questions, share what's working, find trusted resources and take a moment for yourself, all in one place.

Raising an autistic child is full of love, learning and moments only other families truly understand. United For Autism brings those families together.

COMMUNITY SPACES
• Join topic-based spaces for everyday questions, sensory needs, school and therapy, milestones and more
• Share your experiences, ask for advice and reply to other families
• Post anonymously whenever you need extra privacy
• Like and save helpful posts to come back to later

CALM KIT
• Build your own kit of sensory and regulation tools that help your child feel safe
• Note why each tool works and when to use it
• Discover what has helped other families most, and share your own ideas

EMOTIONAL SUPPORT
• A gentle space for caregiver wellbeing
• Respond to reflection prompts and share honestly. Shares are anonymous.
• Receive kind words from people who understand
• Daily affirmations to remind you of the unseen work you do

LEARN
• Easy-to-read articles on autism, communication, routines, sensory processing and caregiving
• Community members can contribute articles from their own experience

EVENTS
• Find online and in-person meetups, workshops and support groups
• RSVP and keep track of what's coming up
• Host your own event for local families

FIND A THERAPIST
• Browse therapists by specialisation, city and experience
• Read profiles to help you find the right support for your family

CONNECT WITH OTHER FAMILIES
• Explore the members directory and connect with other caregivers
• Control your profile visibility and privacy settings

BUILT WITH CARE
• Community guidelines that keep conversations kind and respectful
• Privacy controls let you choose what others see
• Delete your account and your data at any time from the app

Whether you have a new diagnosis or years of experience, you are not alone. Join United For Autism and find your people.

Important: United For Autism is a peer-support and information app. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional about your child's health and care. If you or someone you care for is in crisis, contact your local emergency services.
